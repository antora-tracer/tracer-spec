= Architecture Decision Record

An ADR records a costly-to-reverse software architecture choice, its context, alternatives, selected option, rationale, and consequences. Use `DEC` for domain or business decisions; do not create an ADR for every design choice.

Resolve material open choices with the user before finalizing an ADR. Link it to the change, relevant requirements, and architecture items it decides.

== Item template

[source,asciidoc]
----
[#ADR-NNN, item, role=adr, status=proposed, title="<architecture decision>"]
--
Context:: Why the choice is needed.
Options:: Material alternatives with advantages and disadvantages.
Decision:: The selected option.
Rationale:: Why it was selected.
Consequences:: Resulting benefits, costs, and constraints.

recorded_by:CHG-NNN[]
addresses:REQ-NNN[]
decides:ARC-NNN[]
tracer:links[]
--
----
