= Change Design

A design item records the technical approach for one refined implementation change. Create it after refining software requirements and before creating temporary execution tasks. Keep the design after the change closes; tasks remain under `tspec/<change-id>/` and are removed by archive.

Link the design to its change, relevant requirements, affected architecture items, and related ADRs. Record material alternatives and trade-offs or state explicitly that the change has no architecture impact.

== Item template

[source,asciidoc]
----
[#DSN-NNN, item, role=design, status=proposed, title="Design for CHG-NNN: <change>"]
--
Context:: Requirements and architectural context.
Approach:: How the change realizes the requirements.
Architecture impact:: Affected ARC items and proposed updates, or none.
Alternatives and trade-offs:: Material choices and consequences.

recorded_by:CHG-NNN[]
realizes:REQ-NNN[]
affects:ARC-NNN[]
references:ADR-NNN[]
tracer:links[]
--
----
