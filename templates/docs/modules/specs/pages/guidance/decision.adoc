= Decision

A *Decision* records a domain or specification choice: its context, the option chosen, and the consequences. Costly-to-reverse software architecture choices belong in an ADR instead.

Status: `proposed` → `accepted` → `rejected` · `superseded`.

== Item template

[source,asciidoc]
----
[#DEC-NNN, item, role=decision, status=proposed, title="<decision>"]
--
Context:: Why this decision is needed.
Options:: Material alternatives and their advantages and disadvantages.
Decision:: What was decided.
Rationale:: Why the chosen option was selected.
Consequences:: What follows.

recorded_by:CHG-NNN[]
tracer:links[]
--
----
