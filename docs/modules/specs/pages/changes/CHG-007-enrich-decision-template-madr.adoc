[#CHG-007, item, role=change, status=closed, title="Enrich the decision template with MADR-style fields"]
--
Rationale:: The decision role records context, the selected option, and consequences.
It leaves out the alternatives and the reasons for choosing one, so readers cannot reconstruct the decision.
MADR's body structure records the options considered, their pros and cons, the selected option, and the rationale.
It applies to decisions beyond architecture.
Use MADR's fields in the AsciiDoc labeled-list template.
Leave out the Status, Deciders, Date, and Technical Story header; the item's status attribute, its `recorded_by` relation, and `git` already express them.

Outcome:: The decision guidance template lists Context, Options (with each option's good and bad points nested beneath it), Decision, Rationale, and Consequences.
Future decisions will record the options considered, the option chosen, why it was chosen, and the trade-offs.

Out-of-scope:: Markdown files.
The core repository's `adr.adoc`.
Rewriting the existing DEC-001 to DEC-006 items, which remain historical records.
A Drivers field can wait until decisions need an explicit force list.
