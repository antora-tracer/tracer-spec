== Antora Tracer Roles

Our basic model for a traced and AI-friendly workflow knows the following roles

[cols="1,3a"]
|===
|Role | Description

|Business Goal
|A **business goal** is a desired outcome that an organization aims to achieve to support its strategic direction and business success.

|User Need
|A **user need** describes what a user requires from a system to achieve a desired goal or perform a task.
It focuses on the user’s desired outcome rather than prescribing a specific solution or implementation.

|Quality Attribute
|A **quality attribute** is a measurable or assessable characteristic of a system that describes how well it performs or satisfies a requirement, rather than what functionality it provides. Examples include performance, security, reliability, usability, and maintainability.

|Quality Scenario
|A **quality scenario** describes a specific, testable situation in which a system’s quality attribute is evaluated.
It defines the relevant context, stimulus, expected system response, and measurable response criteria.

|Use Case
|A **Use Case** describes how an actor interacts with a system to achieve a specific goal.
It defines the expected behavior of the system from the actor’s perspective, including the relevant interactions and outcomes.

|Requirement
|Anything that describes what observable behaviour the software should expose.
A **requirement** can be a functional, a constraint, or a non-functional requirement.
A requirement can also be against processes or tooling for building and delivering the software.

|Design Element
|A **Design Element** is the resulting concrete structural or behavioral element, such as a component, interface, data model, or deployment element.

|Decision Record
|When you have to decide between multiple alternatives, you should document your decision process.
Typical examples are Architecture Decision Records or Requirement Decision Records.
|===
