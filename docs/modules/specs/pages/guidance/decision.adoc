= Decision

A *Decision* records a domain or specification choice: its context, the option chosen, and the consequences.
Use an ADR for a costly-to-reverse software architecture choice.

Status: `proposed` → `accepted` → `rejected` · `superseded`.

== Placement

Write each decision as its own partial under `docs/modules/specs/partials/decisions/` and include it from `docs/modules/specs/pages/decisions.adoc`.
That page groups items by status: an `accepted` decision sits under `== Accepted` and governs the current workflow; a `proposed` one sits under `== Proposed`.
Write a decision as `proposed`, and move it to `== Accepted` and `status=accepted` once it takes effect.
A superseded decision moves to `partials/decisions/superseded.adoc` instead of staying among the live items.

== Item template

[source,asciidoc]
----
[#DEC-NNN, item, role=decision, status=proposed, title="<domain or specification choice>"]
--
Context:: Why this decision is needed.
Options:: Material alternatives and their advantages and disadvantages.
Decision:: What was decided.
Rationale:: Why the chosen option was selected.
Consequences:: What follows.

recorded_by:CHG-NNN[]
tracer:links[]
--
----
