= Change Design

include::partial$documentation/DOC-013-design-guidance.adoc[]

A *Design* records the technical approach for one implementation change. It is durable and remains with the change after closure; execution tasks remain temporary under `tspec/<change-id>/`.

Create one design item during `tspec-refine-change`, after the software requirements and before the task list. Link the change, relevant requirements, affected architecture items, and any related ADRs. Record material alternatives and trade-offs; for a change with no architecture impact, say so explicitly.

== Placement

Write each design as its own partial under `docs/modules/specs/partials/designs/` and include it from `docs/modules/specs/pages/designs.adoc`.
That page gives each design its own `== CHG-NNN — <change>` section, titled with the change the design records, so the design appears in the page's contents list.
Titling the section with the change rather than the design keeps the page keyed by the change a reader arrives with; the design's own title renders as a block title and does not reach the contents list.
A superseded design moves to `partials/designs/superseded.adoc` instead of staying among the live items.

A design MAY carry a diagram when its approach is structural — an ordered pipeline, a branch, or a guard sequence that prose forces a reader to reconstruct.
Add it only then, not as decoration: keep a design whose approach is three mechanical steps or a considered trade-off as prose.
Keep the source as `<DSN-id>-<slug>.puml` under `docs/modules/specs/examples/diagrams/` and reference it from the design body with a diagram block whose body is the include, matching the architecture guidance.

== Item template

[source,asciidoc]
----
[#DSN-NNN, item, role=design, status=proposed, title="Design for CHG-NNN: <change>"]
--
Context:: The requirements and architectural context for this change.
Approach:: How the change realizes its requirements.
Architecture impact:: The affected ARC items and proposed updates, or none.
Alternatives and trade-offs:: Material options and their consequences.

recorded_by:CHG-NNN[]
realizes:REQ-NNN[]
affects:ARC-NNN[]
references:ADR-NNN[]
tracer:links[]
--
----

Keep details proportional to the change. Revise the design with the user's review when implementation changes its approach.
