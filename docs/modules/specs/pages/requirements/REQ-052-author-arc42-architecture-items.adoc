[#REQ-052, item, role=requirement, level=software, status=approved, title="Author traceable architecture elements"]
--
The system SHALL let authors record each independently meaningful architecture element as a durable `architecture` item with a unique `ARC` identifier, a `kind` value, and links to related requirements, changes, and ADRs.
The architecture documentation SHALL present those items within the applicable arc42 section.

Scenario:: Given a contributor records a cross-cutting concept, when it is published, then it appears as an ARC item classified as a cross-cutting concept in the arc42-aligned baseline and its links resolve.
