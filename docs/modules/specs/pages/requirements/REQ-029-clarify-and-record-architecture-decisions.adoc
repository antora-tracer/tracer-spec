[#REQ-029, item, role=requirement, level=component, status=approved, title="Resolve and trace architecture decisions"]
--
When a change design leaves a material architecture choice unresolved, the workflow SHALL request the user's decision before finalizing the design.
The chosen option SHALL be recorded in an ADR linked to the change, relevant requirements, and affected architecture items.
