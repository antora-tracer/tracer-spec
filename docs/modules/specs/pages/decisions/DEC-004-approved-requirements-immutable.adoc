[#DEC-004, item, role=decision, status=accepted, title="Approved requirements are immutable, gated on meaning"]
--
Context:: In-place edits break the referential integrity of the traceability graph.
Decision:: An approved requirement is immutable: a change to its meaning supersedes it with a new requirement. Typos may be edited in place.
Consequences:: Append-only requirements with a supersedes lineage edge; some document sprawl.
